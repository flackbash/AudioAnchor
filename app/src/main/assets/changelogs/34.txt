• Added home screen widgets: a play button that resumes your most recently played track, and larger ones with skip buttons and a bookmark button

• Track titles are now sorted naturally, so "Track 2" comes before "Track 10"

• Pressing play on a finished track now restarts it from the beginning, with a themed replay icon on the button

• Playback position is now saved periodically while playing, so progress is no longer lost if the app is killed in the background

• The play queue used for autoplay and skip now matches the order tracks are shown in, including pinned tracks and custom sort orders

• Library sync is faster, especially for long .webm/.mkv files, and no longer creates duplicate entries in your library

• .webm/.mkv/.3gp files now stay visible under Android's newer media permissions, with an explanation shown before the permission is requested

• Fixed several playback issues
